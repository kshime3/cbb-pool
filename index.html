Fine print

Ties share a rank and split the prize money for the places they cover.

Salary cap $101. Scores come from ESPN and are checked every morning.

Projections use ESPN's Matchup Predictor (BPI) win odds for every remaining scheduled game: each game adds its chance of a win × 2 minus its chance of a loss × 1. Games against non-Division I teams count as 95% wins. Conference tournaments are added once they're scheduled in March. Just for fun: prize money goes by actual points.
